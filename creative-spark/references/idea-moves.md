# Idea moves

Use 1 move at a time. Write 3 answers before you judge any of them.

## Concept moves
- **Remove:** what happens if the most expected part is gone? No sign-up wall. No menu. No dashboard.
- **Reverse:** show the result first and the input second. Let the app go first.
- **Combine:** join this app with 1 thing from another field. A budget that reads like a weather report.
- **Exaggerate:** take the 1 thing users love and make it 10 times bigger.
- **Shrink:** do the job in 1 screen, 1 tap, or 1 sentence.
- **Personify:** if the app were a person or a place, what would it sound and look like?
- **Time:** how does it differ at first open, on day 30, at night, on a deadline?
- **Reward:** what small moment makes the user smile after the main task?

## Feature moves
- Use what the app already knows: the user's own data, history, time, and place.
- Turn a setting into a smart default the user can change.
- Turn a list into a map, a timeline, a board, or a single next step.
- Turn a report into 1 sentence that says what changed and what to do.
- Let the user undo instead of confirm.
- Offer a share, export, or handoff at the moment of success.

## Visual moves
- 1 strong typeface used large, with 1 quiet partner.
- 1 accent color used rarely, so it means something.
- An uneven grid: 1 large item, several small.
- Real photos, drawings, or data from the subject in place of stock art.
- Texture from the subject: paper, fabric, grain, ink, light.
- White space as the main design element.

## Motion moves
- Motion explains: where a thing came from, where it went, what changed.
- 1 entrance for the signature moment. Calm everywhere else.
- Respond within 100 ms to every tap, even if the work takes longer.
- Numbers and charts animate from the old value to the new one.
- Scroll reveals content in reading order, never hides it.

## Test each idea
- Would the user notice it is gone?
- Can you say it in 1 sentence?
- Does it fit only this app?
- Can you build it in 1 slice?
