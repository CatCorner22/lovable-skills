# Craft floor

Every creative choice must pass these checks in the rendered preview.

## Clarity
- The user can tell what the screen is for in 5 seconds.
- 1 primary action per screen, visible in the first viewport.
- Navigation, forms, and core actions sit where users expect them.

## Reading
- Body text is at least 16 px, with line height 1.4–1.6 and lines near 65 characters.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1. Large text and interface parts are at least 3:1.
- Color never carries meaning alone. Pair it with text or an icon.

## Touch and keyboard
- Touch targets are at least 44 px on phones.
- Every action works by keyboard, and focus is visible.
- Dragging has a tap or click alternative.

## Motion
- Motion has a purpose and lasts under 400 ms for interface changes.
- Honor the reduced-motion setting.
- Nothing flashes, loops without end, or blocks the user's next action.

## States and speed
- Loading, empty, error, success, and long-text states all fit the concept.
- The signature idea adds no layout shift and no slow first load. Load heavy media late.
- The design holds at 390 px and 1440 px.

## Honesty
- Show real costs and real progress. Make cancel and change easy.
- Mark example data as example data.

If you cannot check an item in the preview, say which one.
