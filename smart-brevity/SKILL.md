# Smart Brevity

Write short, not shallow. Keep the facts the reader needs to act.

## Before writing
Name 1 audience and the 1 thing they must remember or do. Infer and label a cheap assumption if the draft makes the answer clear.

## Structure
1. **Tease:** at most 6 words. Make a point, not a label.
2. **Lede:** 1 sentence with the newest, most important fact or ask. Make it work alone.
3. **Why it matters:** 1–2 sentences on why this reader needs it now.
4. Add only the signposts that earn their place:
   - **The big picture:** context.
   - **Driving the news:** the trigger.
   - **By the numbers:** 2–4 figures.
   - **Between the lines:** a supported inference.
   - **Yes, but:** risks and caveats.
   - **What they're saying:** a short, sourced quote.
   - **What's next** or **What we need:** actions, owners, dates.
   - **Go deeper:** useful source links.
   - **The bottom line:** an optional 1-sentence close.

## Line edit
Use active voice, concrete words, and digits. Give each sentence 1 idea. Put 3 or more items in parallel bullets, each near 2 lines or fewer. Lead with the strong word or number.

Cut “very”, “really”, “in order to”, jargon, throat-clearing, and repeated points. Bold signposts and at most 1 key phrase per section. Keep whitespace. Use a table or chart only when it beats words.

## Fit the format
- Email or team announcement: make the tease the subject; aim for 1 phone screen.
- Board update: lead with the result or decision; show numbers, caveats, and the ask.
- Memo: lead with the recommendation; end with owners and dates.
- Newsletter: use 1 block per item.
- Meeting brief: name the decision and pre-reads.
- Presentation: use 1 idea and at most 3 bullets per slide.

## Check and return
Compare the revision with its source for facts, numbers, owners, dates, caveats, and links. Keep uncertainty visible. The format shapes the message; it does not decide the facts.

Return the rewrite first. Mention a material cut in 1 line. Stop when the reader has what they need.

Example:
**Migration moves to March 18**
Go-live moves 2 weeks so IT can fix a payment-file error.
**Why it matters:** Finance must use the old system for March 4 payments.
**What's next:** IT must fix and retest the file by March 11.
