# Adams policy drafting

Apply A Manual of Style for Contract Drafting, 5th ed., adapted for one-way codes. This skill gives drafting help, not legal advice. A lawyer should review the text before use.

## 1. Gather the facts
Read the actual app flow. List the operator, users, data, vendors, payments, cancellation path, retention rules, and jurisdiction. Ask for missing legal or business choices in 1 batch. Mark each gap `[X1: owner must supply …]`. Keep the draft unpublished while material gaps remain.

## 2. Draft one rule at a time
1. Name the actor as the subject of an active verb in every sentence, including headings and table cells.
2. Use **must** for a duty, **must not** for a prohibition, and **may** for permission. Use **does not have to** for the absence of a duty. Use present tense for facts. Replace “shall”; distinguish ability from permission.
3. Define necessary terms once. Use 1 term for 1 meaning. Use common words elsewhere.
4. Use digits for numbers. State units, currency, time zone, and the event that starts each period.
5. Put each condition next to its rule. State the outcome if the condition fails.
6. State whether a list means any item or all items. Replace “and/or” with the intended choice. Give “only” and exceptions a clear scope.
7. Name who acts, what they do, when they do it, and how the user makes a request. Replace vague deadlines only with owner-approved values.

Preserve each right, duty, exception, and legal effect when revising. Flag a proposed meaning change for approval. Keep any legally required verbatim wording intact and flag its conflict with the style rule for counsel. Verify the provision before treating wording as legally required. Supply no invented citation or compliance claim.

## 3. Match the app
Compare the draft with consent controls, data collection, account deletion, billing, refunds, and cancellation. Separate optional consent from required terms. Confirm that links and contact paths work. Ask counsel to resolve conflicts between law, product behavior, and policy text.

Return the draft, missing decisions, meaning changes, and lawyer-review items. Mark the review date and policy version. Report checks you ran, not legal approval.
