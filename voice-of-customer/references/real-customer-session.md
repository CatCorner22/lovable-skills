# Real customer session

Use this with 3–5 real people per milestone. Each session takes 15 minutes.

## Before
- Pick people who match the customers in `docs/customers.md`.
- Share the preview link. Use a draft or example data, never live customer data.
- Choose 2–3 tasks. Phrase each as a goal, not as steps: "Book a visit for next week", not "Click Book, then pick a date".

## During
1. Say: "We are testing the app, not you. Please think out loud."
2. Give 1 task at a time. Stay quiet. Do not help or explain.
3. Note the exact words they use, where they pause, where they go wrong, and what they expected.
4. If they get stuck for 1 minute, ask "What are you looking for?" and note the answer.

## After each task, ask
- What did you expect to happen there?
- What was the hardest part?
- What word or label confused you?

## At the end, ask
- What would you use this for?
- What is missing?
- What would make you stop using it?

## Bring it back
Paste the notes into the Lovable chat. Lovable then:
- Adds the quotes and behaviors to `docs/customers.md`, marked **real**.
- Replaces any assumed line that the real evidence contradicts.
- Lists the issues that 2 or more people hit. Those come first.
- Reruns the 3 test passes against the updated customers.
